My work sits at the intersection of casework and reporting: supervising probation and parole cases on the ground, and separately, building the regional performance reviews that compare how the region's twelve field offices are actually doing — submission timeliness, officer caseload, supervision outcomes.

Before joining the Parole and Probation Administration, I spent several years in BPO operations, which is where the comfort with data, process discipline, and clear reporting under deadline came from. Law and criminology gave me the subject matter; that background gave me the habit of turning raw case data into something a supervisor can act on in one read.
